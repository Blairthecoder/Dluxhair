Booking policies

Deposit

Every service requires a non-refundable $25 deposit. It is credited toward your appointment.

Late policy

Please arrive on time. There is a 15-minute grace period, then a $25 late fee applies.

Cancellations

Cancel at least 24 hours before your appointment. No-call, no-shows are charged half the service fee.

Guests

No extra guests. Clients 16 and up may come alone; clients under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

Styling add-ons

If you want any extra styling or service, such as curling, crimping, a hair wash or cutting, add it as an “add-on” when you book.

Natural hair prep

Unless you are paying for an extra service, your natural hair must not be braided and must be free of any glue. Shampoo is free of charge. Take-down is an add-on.

Hair drop-offs

All drop-offs must include your name and appointment date and time. Email or text to schedule a drop-off.

After hours

Booking after 7 PM carries an after-hours fee. Text with your preferred time, date and style if the site won't let you book it.
