Before your appointment

  • Hair is not provided for most braid styles. Bring your own hair or select the hair add-on when booking.
  • Miracle Knots need a 4-bag minimum. Human hair is available as an add-on in natural colors only.
  • Boho styles come in basic colors only.
  • Come with your hair free of braids and glue unless you booked a take-down. Shampoo is included free of charge.
  • Need old braids removed first? Book Braid Take Down & Shampoo (detangle, exfoliate, cleanse and blow out) or add take-down to your appointment.
  • Dropping off hair ahead of time? Label it with your name and appointment date and time, and text or email to schedule the drop-off.

Good to know

How long do knotless braids take?

Times on the menu run from about 2 hours for jumbo knotless to 5 hours for small knotless. Boho and layered styles take longer than plain knotless in the same size.

Can I get just the front or back redone?

Yes. Book Knotless Touch-Up (Top/Back), starting at $125.

Do you braid kids or men?

Yes. See kids braids and men's braids & twists for those menus and prices.

What colors are available for boho and Miracle Knots?

Boho styles are basic colors only. Miracle Knots with human hair are natural colors only.
