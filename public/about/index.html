Specialties

What Denise does

Knotless braids

Every size from small to jumbo, boho knotless, Miracle Knots, feed-ins and soft locs.

Installs & wigs

Sew-ins, closure, frontal and 360 installs, glueless units, pixies and wig installs.

Silk press & natural hair

Silk press, color, highlights, relaxers and twists for natural hair.

The studio

Appointments are one-on-one and booked online. To keep things running on time, the studio has a 15-minute grace period, a 24-hour cancellation window and a no-extra-guests policy.
