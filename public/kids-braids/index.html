Braids and styles sized for kids

The kids menu has its own pricing and shorter appointment times for knotless braids, box braids (rubber band method), bohemian and lemonade braids, feed-ins, braided ponytails and crochet styles. Silk press, relaxer, sleek ponytail and a cleanse shampoo are also available.

Kids policy: clients under 16 must be accompanied by an adult. No extra guests, please.

Before your appointment

  • One adult must stay with clients under 16. No extra guests.
  • Hair is not provided for Kids Miracle Knots (2-bag minimum). Choose the hair add-on if needed.
  • Come with your hair free of braids and glue unless you booked a take-down. Shampoo is included free of charge.
  • Bring snacks or a quiet activity for longer appointments.

Good to know

Is there an age limit?

Clients under 16 are welcome with an adult present. Clients 16 and up may come alone.

How long do kids braids take?

Most kids styles take 2 to 3 hours 45 minutes depending on the style. A kids dread twist takes about 45 minutes.

Can my child get a silk press?

Yes. Kids silk press starts at $90 and takes about 1 hour.
