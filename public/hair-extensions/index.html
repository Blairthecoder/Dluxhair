Before your appointment

  • Bringing your own bundles, closure, frontal or wig? Hair drop-offs must include your name and appointment date and time. Text or email to schedule the drop-off.
  • Come with your hair free of braids and glue unless you booked a take-down. Shampoo is included free of charge.
  • Wearing an old install? Book Weave Sew/Bond Take Down & Shampoo or add take-down so your appointment has enough time.
  • Want curls, crimping, a wash or a cut? Add it as an “add-on” when you book so enough time is reserved.

Good to know

What's the difference between a closure and a frontal install?

A closure covers the top of the head, while a frontal runs ear to ear along the hairline, and a 360 frontal goes all the way around. Closure installs start at $190, frontal installs at $225 and 360 frontal installs at $250.

Do you offer glueless installs?

Yes. Glueless traditional quick weave and glueless closure installs start at $175, glueless frontal installs at $200 and a 27-piece glueless pixie at $125.

How do I maintain my install?

Book a Closure/Frontal Install Touch-Up ($100), Basic Sew-In Shampoo/Style Touch-Up ($90) or Refresh Styling ($80) between installs.

Do you do tape-in extensions?

Yes. Tape-ins are done with a silk press, starting at $600.
