Frequently Asked Questions

Booking, deposits, prep and policy answers for Dlux Beauty clients.

  1. Home
  2. FAQ

Booking & deposits

How do I book an appointment?

Book online through the booking page. Choose your service category, then your style and time. If you can’t find the time you need, call or text 512-810-4307.

Do I need a deposit?

Yes. All services require a non-refundable $25 deposit. The deposit is credited toward your appointment.

Can I book after hours?

Appointments after 7 PM carry an after-hours fee. If the booking page won't let you book the time you want, pick any open spot and text 512-810-4307 with your preferred date, time and style. You can also send your deposit by Zelle or Cash App with the date, time and style.

Do you sell gift certificates?

Yes. Gift certificates are available through the online booking page.

Policies

What is your late policy?

Please arrive on time. There is a 15-minute grace period. After that, a $25 late fee applies.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancellations must be made at least 24 hours before your appointment. No-call, no-shows are charged half the service fee.

Can I bring a guest?

No extra guests are allowed. Clients 16 and up may come alone. Clients under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

Do you do kids' hair?

Yes. There is a separate kids menu. Clients under 16 must have an adult with them.

Prep & add-ons

How should I prepare my natural hair?

Unless you booked a take-down or other extra service, come with your hair out of braids and free of any glue. Shampoo is included free of charge. Take-down is an add-on.

Is hair included with braid services?

Not for most braid styles. Bring your own hair or choose the hair add-on when you book. Miracle Knots need a 4-bag minimum (2 bags for kids). See the braid menu.

How do I add curls, a wash or a cut?

Add any extra styling or service, such as curling, crimping, a hair wash or cutting, as an “add-on” when you book. That reserves the extra time.

How do I drop off my hair before my appointment?

All drop-offs must include your name and appointment date and time. Text or email to schedule the drop-off.

Payment & location

What forms of payment do you accept?

Apple Pay, Cash App, Zelle, cash and card.

Where is Dlux Beauty located?

Dlux Beauty is on Murphy Rd in Stafford, TX. Your exact address is in your booking confirmation. See contact & directions.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday 9 AM to 9 PM, Saturday and Sunday 7 AM to 5 PM. Appointments after 7 PM carry an after-hours fee.
